====================================================================
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR SHAPE SELECTION
====================================================================

  A shape is only a valid item if NO closed-form program reproduces it.
  Smooth analytic curves -- spirals, waves, arcs, circles -- all have one.
  They are the worst possible test shapes, and they look the best in a demo.

  Valid families need a program that cannot be written without knowing
  where the pen currently is: random waypoints, irregular staircases,
  paths with varying curvature, and multi-stroke shapes where the pen must
  travel to a specific absolute location between strokes.
